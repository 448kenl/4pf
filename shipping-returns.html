Shipping & Returns

LOGISTICS, CLEAN.

Straight answers on processing, shipping windows, preorders, exchanges, and package issues.

Processing Time

In-stock orders process within 3–5 business days. Drop spikes may extend handling slightly, but every order receives tracking the moment it moves.

Shipping Windows

Domestic orders usually land in 3–7 business days after dispatch. International windows vary by carrier and customs.

Preorders

If a piece is marked preorder, the product page states the production window clearly before purchase.

Returns / Exchanges

Unworn items may be exchanged or returned within 14 days of delivery unless marked final sale or archive release.

Lost Packages

If tracking stalls or marks delivered without arrival, contact support within 5 days so we can open a carrier review.

Final Sale Drops

Some numbered releases, bundles, and archive pieces are final sale due to scarcity and limited inventory.
