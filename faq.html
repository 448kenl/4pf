The answers people look for before they decide whether they are really inside the drop.

Do you restock?

Rarely. Most numbered pieces do not return. Some hidden variants appear later for SMS members only.

How do pieces fit?

Tees and hoodies skew oversized. Review the size guide if you want a cleaner or heavier drape.

When do orders ship?

Standard processing is 3–5 business days for in-stock pieces.

Are drops limited?

Yes. Scarcity is part of the system. Once counts close, archive starts.

How does SMS access work?

Phone subscribers get early alerts, hidden links, secret releases, and archive warnings.

What is a numbered drop?

A release with a fixed unit count or serialized run that will not stay open forever.

Do you release secret drops?

Yes. Some releases never hit public navigation first.

How do bundles work?

Bundles pair key items at a tighter combined price and may include SMS-only extras.
